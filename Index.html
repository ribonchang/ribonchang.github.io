About Me

I am an experienced Research Scientist and Lab Manager specializing in 3D Genomics. My work focuses on developing cutting-edge 3D genomic technologies, constructing 3D gene transcription regulatory networks using AI, and investigating the role of non-coding DNA regulatory elements in cancer transcription.


I have published nearly 40 high-level academic papers in journals such as Nature, Nature Genetics, Nucleic Acids Research, and Nature Communications, with over 2,000 citations. My work on genome-wide promoter interactions has been highlighted by Faculty of 1000. I hold 14 patents (9 authorized) and have led multiple national-level research projects funded by the NSFC and National Key R&D Programs.
